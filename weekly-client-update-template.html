Friday afternoon gone to writing client updates again?

Weekly Client Update Template: 2-Minute Friday Email With a ChatGPT Prompt

By Mrs. Autopilot · 2 min · No coding

Every Friday it's the same: one update per client, from scratch, trying to remember everything you did this week and make it sound organised. It eats the last good hours of your week.

You already have the facts in your task list. Let one prompt do the wording: about two minutes per client.

Here's the fix

Clients love a weekly update. Writing one from scratch for every client does not. You already have the facts in your task list, so let a prompt do the wording.

1. Keep a quick note during the week

One note per client: what you finished, what's coming, what you're waiting on. Messy is fine, like "invoices sent, website fix pending, need logo files".

2. On Friday, paste this prompt

Open ChatGPT (a free account works), start a new chat, and paste this with your note at the end:

Turn my rough notes into a short weekly update email for my client. Use three headings: Done this week, Next week, Need from you. Use short bullet points, max 5 per heading. Keep every task, date and number exactly as I wrote them and don't add anything I didn't mention. Tone: friendly, clear, professional. Start with 'Hi [Name],', add a one-line subject, and end with a short sign-off.

My notes: [paste your task list here]

3. Read it before you send

Check that every item is true and nothing was added. Fix names and dates inside your email.

4. Want it different?

Reply in the same chat with "shorter", "more formal", or "put Need from you first". It rewrites in seconds.

5. Save the prompt

Keep it one click away every Friday: a note on your desktop, or a Gmail template (gear icon → See all settings → Advanced → Templates → Enable → Save Changes).

A note on privacy

Don't paste confidential details like full client names, addresses, prices, contract terms, passwords or account numbers. Use placeholders like [Client] and [amount] and fill them in inside your email. In ChatGPT you can use Temporary Chat, and in Settings → Data controls you can turn off "Improve the model for everyone". Always follow your client's and company's rules on AI tools.
