Still rewriting meeting notes long after the meeting ended?

ChatGPT Meeting Notes Prompt: Clean Summary From Rough Notes in 30 Seconds

By Mrs. Autopilot · 30 sec · No coding

The meeting took 30 minutes. Turning your scribbles into something you can actually send takes another 30. Who said what, who's doing which task, by when… and then the follow-up email on top.

Here's the one prompt that does all three for you in about 30 seconds.

Here's the fix

Right after any meeting, copy your messy notes. Typos and half-words are fine.

The prompt

Open ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and paste your notes in with this prompt:

These are my rough meeting notes. Give me: 1) a 3-line summary, 2) action items with who and by when, 3) a short follow-up email to the attendees in a friendly, professional tone. Don't add anything that isn't in my notes.

Read it over, fix names if needed, and hit send. Thirty seconds, done before your next meeting.

Bonus

Save the prompt in a note on your phone so it's always one copy-paste away.

One honest note

If your work has rules about client data, don't paste confidential details into an AI tool. Check with your employer first.
