Staring at a blank email to a client for ten minutes?

ChatGPT Prompt for Client Emails: Turn a Messy Note Into a Polished Email

By Mrs. Autopilot · 20 sec · No coding

You know exactly what you need to say: the file is late, the new date is Friday, sorry. But finding words that sound polite, clear and professional? That's where ten minutes disappear, and you still second-guess it before hitting send.

This copy-paste prompt turns your messy note into a polished client email in about 20 seconds.

Here's the fix

The slow part of a client email isn't the facts, it's the wording. You already know what to say: "file late, new date Friday, sorry." This prompt turns that rough note into a polite, clear email.

1. Open ChatGPT

A free account works. Start a new chat.

2. Paste the prompt

Copy this, paste it in and replace the last line with your note:

Rewrite my rough note as a short, friendly, professional email to a client. Keep it under 100 words. Keep every fact, date and number exactly as I wrote them and don't add any promises or details I didn't give. Tone: warm but clear, like a capable assistant. Start with 'Hi [Name],' and end with a one-line sign-off. Add a short subject line.

My note: [paste your messy version here]

3. Read it before you send

Check names, dates and amounts. You're the one hitting send, so it should sound like you.

4. Want a different tone?

Reply in the same chat with "shorter", "a bit firmer", or "more casual". It rewrites in seconds.

5. Keep it one click away

Save the prompt in a note on your desktop, or as a Gmail template (Settings → See all settings → Advanced → Templates → Enable).

A note on privacy

Don't paste confidential details like full client names, addresses, contract terms, invoice or account numbers. Use placeholders like [Client] and [amount] and fill them in inside your email. In ChatGPT you can use Temporary Chat, and in Settings → Data controls you can turn off "Improve the model for everyone". Always follow your company's rules on AI tools.
