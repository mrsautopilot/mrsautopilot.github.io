How many emails does it take you to book one design consultation?

Client Consultation Booking Page for Interior Designers (Free, Google Calendar)

By Mrs. Autopilot · 5 min · No coding

A new enquiry comes in. You reply, they ask about Thursday, you're on site Thursday, they suggest next week, you check your calendar... Booking one consultation often takes five emails and about fifteen minutes. Ten enquiries a week and that's two and a half hours spent on scheduling instead of designing.

Google Calendar has a free booking page built in, called an appointment schedule. You set your consult hours once, send one link, and the client picks a time that works for both of you.

Here's the fix

1. Create the appointment schedule

Open Google Calendar on a computer → click + Create (top left) → Appointment schedule. The basic version is free with a personal Google account and included in Google Workspace.

2. Set your consult hours

Give it a clear title like "30-min design consultation" and choose the duration. Under General availability, set only the hours you actually want to take calls, for example Tue and Thu, 10:00–12:00, so site visits and design time stay protected. Check the time zone.

3. Ask the right questions up front

Under Booking form you can add questions. Useful ones for designers: project address or area, which rooms, rough budget range and ideal start date. You'll arrive at the call already knowing whether it's a fit. Add a video link or phone number under Booked appointment settings, then click Save. Times that already have events in your calendar won't show as free.

4. Share one link

Click your appointment schedule in the calendar → Share → Copy link. Put it in your enquiry reply, your email signature and your website contact page. New bookings appear in your calendar automatically.

5. A friendly reply to new enquiries

Open Claude or ChatGPT (a free account works), start a new chat and paste this prompt:

Write a short, warm reply (under 70 words) to a new interior design enquiry. Thank them, say I'd love to hear about their project, and invite them to book a 30-minute consultation using my booking link. Professional, no pressure. Use placeholders [Name], [room or project] and [link]. Add a one-line subject.

Save the reply as a Gmail template so every new enquiry gets an answer in two clicks.

Good to know

Some extras (for example checking several calendars at once or extra reminders) are only on paid Google Workspace plans, and menus can differ slightly between versions. Using Outlook? Microsoft 365 has a similar feature called Bookings with me, depending on your plan.

A note on privacy

Anyone with the link can see when you're free (not what your other events are) and can book a slot, so share it where real enquiries come from. When you use AI for the reply text, keep the placeholders and add names yourself.
